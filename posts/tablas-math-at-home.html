Tablas: A Maths Practice Website for My Son

October 6, 2026 1 min read

I wanted a maths game my son could use at home. Instead of searching for a safe game I felt comfortable giving him, I used AI tools to build a small website.

That is what I like about these tools: they make little projects like this easy to put together. I knew what I wanted — pick an operation, set up a short round, and practise — and AI helped me turn it into a working page quickly.

I still needed to check that it worked and decide what belonged in it. But building a simple game for my son was now a quick, practical option. I could make the thing we needed, then adjust it as we used it.
